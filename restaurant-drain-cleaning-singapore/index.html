Build a package around your kitchen

The useful starting point is your kitchen’s layout and fault history. Note which sinks or traps cause problems and whether an issue appears only during service. Discuss the tasks, frequency and access arrangements for each visit so the maintenance scope is easy for your team to follow.

Before you request a quote

Send the outlet address, operating hours, number of kitchen areas and existing maintenance records. Highlight repeated blockages and any limits on access before opening or after closing. Ask which routine tasks are included and how additional repairs or urgent call-outs would be quoted.

A common question

Does the package include other cleaning work?

The published F&B offering also lists duct and hood cleaning, high-level cleaning and odour control. If you need these alongside plumbing maintenance, include them in your enquiry and confirm the exact tasks in the proposed package.
