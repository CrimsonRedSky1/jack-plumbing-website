The service

How we can help

TNE Plumbing supports commercial drainage systems with floor trap, sink, toilet and kitchen line clearing. The service scope includes high-pressure cleaning, grease trap flushing, and sump pit desilting, vacuuming and cleaning. Planned water jetting is also available for sites with recurring drainage issues.

  • Kitchen drainage and grease trap cleaning
  • Floor traps, sinks and toilet drainage
  • Sump pits in basements and car parks

Look at the drainage system as a whole

A building with multiple affected outlets needs a clear picture of where the problems occur. For property teams, a simple list of recurring faults by floor or tenancy can make a service enquiry more useful. Include whether the symptoms appear during peak use or continue throughout the day.

Before you request a quote

Share a site plan if available, the affected outlets, recent cleaning records and access arrangements for shared areas. For kitchen work, include your operating hours and the location of grease traps. Ask for the proposed cleaning areas and any exclusions to be identified in the quote.

A common question

How should we plan repeat cleaning?

Use the site’s usage and blockage history to discuss a suitable interval. A busy kitchen and a lightly used office do not place the same demand on drains. Review the plan if problems begin recurring between visits.
