The service

How we can help

TNE Plumbing provides high-pressure water jetting and hydro jetting for commercial kitchens, industrial premises and managed properties. Water jets loosen deposits inside drainage pipes, including grease, food debris and sediment. Jetting can be considered for an urgent blockage or as part of planned pipe cleaning.

  • Grease build-up in kitchen drainage lines
  • Repeated chokes after basic drain clearing
  • Sludge and sediment in larger drainage runs

When clearing the opening is not enough

A drain may begin flowing again while deposits remain along the pipe walls. If the same line keeps choking, discuss whether a deeper clean is appropriate. The pipe condition, access points and type of obstruction matter when selecting a cleaning method; jetting does not repair a broken pipe.

Before you request a quote

Send the location of the affected drain, whether other outlets are backing up, and a brief history of previous clearing work. Photos of accessible inspection points and the route into the site help explain the job. For a business, include opening hours and any limits on working around customers.

A common question

Is jetting the same as choke clearing?

Jetting is a cleaning method; choke clearing describes the problem being addressed. The appropriate method depends on the obstruction and pipe condition. Tell us if earlier clearing only helped for a short time.
