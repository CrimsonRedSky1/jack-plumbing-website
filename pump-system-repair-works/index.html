The service

How we can help

TNE Plumbing provides water and sewage pump system repair and maintenance. Faults may involve leaks, worn seals, impellers or other components. The condition of the pump and the nature of the failure determine whether a component repair or a wider replacement is appropriate.

  • Water pump malfunctions and leaks
  • Sewage and ejector pump system faults
  • Maintenance and worn component assessment

Repair or replacement starts with diagnosis

A pump fault does not automatically mean the whole unit needs replacing. Equally, repeat failures can make another isolated repair less useful. Discuss the fault history, service records and available parts when comparing options, and ask how the proposed work addresses the symptom you reported.

Before you request a quote

Send the make and model or a clear photo of the nameplate, the pump’s purpose and a description of the problem. Include when it last worked normally, any displayed alarm message, and previous repairs. Let us know about restricted access to the pump room or pit.

A common question

How often should a pump be serviced?

Start with the manufacturer’s maintenance guidance and discuss the actual usage and operating conditions. Share existing service records so an appropriate maintenance plan can be considered for your equipment.
